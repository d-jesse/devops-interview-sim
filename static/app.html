Before you start

  • Answer like you're speaking out loud in a real interview — explain your reasoning, not just a one-word answer.
  • Be specific: name the actual commands, flags, or tools you'd use (e.g. kubectl get pods -A), not just "I'd check the pods."
  • Not sure? Say what you'd try and why — reasoning counts toward your score.
  • Once you move to the next question you can't go back, so take your time.
